# Private-preview content audit — 31 August 2026

## Result

The preview has useful, clearly bounded material to test the reading experience. It does **not** have 100 Syāt-published news stories, because generating or publishing them from RSS headlines would be unsafe and misleading.

| Material | Count | Review result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| India-first source signals | 100 | Dated, original HTTPS links, `needs_editorial_review`, and `link_only` |
| Automated source publishers in this snapshot | 3 | The Hindu, The Indian Express, and Mongabay India |
| Individual RSS feeds in this snapshot | 9 | Taken in turns so one fast feed does not fill the queue |
| Timeless starting questions | 100 | Unique, question-shaped, spread across at least 10 themes |
| Visible teaching fixtures | 2 | Explicitly fictional Indian contexts, source cards, evidence labels, at least two standpoints |
| Rights-cleared external media | 0 | Correctly absent; authored diagrams only |
| Live AI drafts stored or published | 0 | Correctly absent |

## What was checked

- The source queue has exactly 100 dated HTTPS entries and every entry is link-only and waiting for editorial review.
- The source collection code caps each feed and then takes turns across feeds before the queue reaches 100.
- The home and Explore pages never present a source signal as Syāt reporting. Sensitive event titles do not become casual home-page teasers.
- Timeless topics are static questions, not unsupported answers. Their IDs and slugs are unique.
- Fixtures name their fictional scope, their sources, their perspective limits, and their illustration credit and limitation.
- The OpenRouter client was tested with a fictional India-context source pack. It returned a review-only structured draft, then passed the automatic review with warnings for a one-publisher pack and a proposed media item. The output was deliberately discarded.

## Important limits

- This snapshot is source-diverse by feed and section, but not a claim of political balance. Syāt does not rate publishers as left, right, or centre. ThePrint, The Wire, and Scroll are set up as manual, link-only editor picks until a stable permitted feed is confirmed.
- An original headline is not enough evidence for a Syāt story. A selected item needs a source dossier, a written India connection, claim-by-claim review, and a human editor’s approval.
- No third-party image, video, or social embed has been published. An official embed is a future candidate only after the source, terms, creator credit, accessibility text, and reviewer approval are recorded.

## Before public use

An editor must approve the source pack and wording; a rights reviewer must approve every external asset; and a Jain scholar or practitioner must review the About wording before release.
