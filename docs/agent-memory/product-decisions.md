# Product decisions

## 2026-08-31 — first build brief

- Syāt is a reading-first news and context product shaped by anekāntavāda and syādvāda: it makes room for more than one well-supported perspective.
- The main page focuses on reading. Reframe stays as a quiet tab and dedicated page.
- Mobile navigation is Home, Explore, Reframe, Saved, and You. Home includes a News/Timeless switch.
- English and Hindi are the first two editions.
- Build three connected visual directions from the same content system: Annotated Evidence, Warm Commons, and Signal Garden. The chosen public direction will be selected after review; Warm Commons is the current accessibility and comfort baseline.
- Onboarding must explain the product in plain language and guide the first reading, save, source view, and Reframe visit.
- The first-use guide is four ordered browser-local steps. Completion uses the versioned `syat:onboarding:v1:completed` marker; storage failures never stop a reader from reaching the teaching story.
- Both published teaching fixtures share one server-rendered story body. Each ends with a real source anchor, an honest saved-account gate, a Task-2-compatible Reframe query, and a related catalogue subject.
- Media must be commercial-safe and credited. Social content uses official embeds only.
- Preview stays private, watermarked, and `noindex` until a human approves publication.
- Reading posts show a source-bound timeline, named/fictitious people-or-roles connections, statement basis and limits, and a News-to-Timeless Context Bridge. A time may be exact, a period, or explicitly unknown; names are never presented as social profiles. Fixture media stays route-specific and visibly not publishable until a person clears its source, credit, rights, and review record.
- Publisher RSS is private intake metadata, not automatic public reading content. The reviewed public allow-list starts empty and fails closed when the snapshot is stale, invalid, or in the future. Static fixture documents, not the About page, explain the fictional teaching sources. Studio shows actual publisher concentration and acquisition gaps instead of claiming balance.
- The current private source queue has 59 records after a complete, single permitted collection: 24 The Indian Express, 24 The Hindu, and 11 Mongabay India. It may remain below 100 until a person approves more permitted intake paths. Candidate data must pass all source-contract checks before atomically replacing the last good queue; local “source pack ready” is a reversible research state, never publication approval.
- Reference reviewed: `anekantavada.lovable.app`. Borrow the clear learning frame — multiple standpoints, intellectual humility, and transparent evidence; say plainly that this is not “both sides” or relativism. A small overlapping-views motif may support the philosophy/About surface. Do not copy its “See the Whole Elephant” hero, soft generic gradient, capsule copy, credibility/disagreement scores, or assets. Syāt remains India-first, source-bound, rounded yet editorial, with its own serif/sans voice and evidence/limits rather than scores that suggest false precision.
